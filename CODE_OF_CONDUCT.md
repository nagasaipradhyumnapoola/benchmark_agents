# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome contributors of all backgrounds. Critique ideas and code with evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews and project-related community interactions. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn contributors, restrict participation or ban repeated or serious misconduct. Apply decisions fairly and protect reporters' privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through their published contact channel. Do not post private personal information in a public issue. If no private channel is available, request a private contact without describing sensitive details publicly.
